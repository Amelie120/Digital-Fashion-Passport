How it works

Three steps between a receipt and a record.

01

Connect your wallet 👛

MetaMask on Sepolia. Your address becomes the name on the passport — no account, no password.

02

Fill in the page ✍️

Maison, item, category, and a link to the proof — a receipt, a care label photo, an authentication card.

03

Push it on-chain 🔗

One transaction mints the entry as an ERC-721. Resell the piece and the passport transfers with it.
