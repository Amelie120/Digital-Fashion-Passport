How it works

Three steps between a receipt and a record.

01

Connect your wallet 👛

MetaMask on Sepolia. Your address becomes the name on the passport — no account, no password.

02

Fill in the page ✍️

Maison, item, category, and a photo. Add a proof link if you have a receipt or an authentication card.

03

Save and lock 🔒

One transaction saves the entry as an ERC-721. Sell the piece and the passport transfers with it.

You'll need a digital wallet 👛

This is a blockchain app, so there's no username or password. Your wallet is your account — it holds your address and signs anything you save.

  1. Install MetaMask, a free browser extension. It takes about two minutes.
  2. Create a wallet and save your recovery phrase somewhere safe. Never share it with anyone.
  3. In MetaMask, turn on test networks and switch to Sepolia.
  4. Grab some free test ETH from the Sepolia faucet to cover transaction fees.
  5. Come back here and hit Connect Wallet.
Nothing here costs real money. Sepolia is Ethereum's test network and the ETH on it has no value — it exists so people can try things safely.
